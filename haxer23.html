**haxer23** is a rapper, producer, and singer from Yelm, Washington, creating music that blends trap, boom bap, and modern hip-hop into a sound that's both hard-hitting and personal. Inspired by Aaron The Producer and Lil Koda, haxer23 takes a hands-on approach to every release, combining original production, melodic vocals, and sharp lyricism to bring each vision to life. On March 17, haxer23 released **Saint Patrick's World**, a Saint Patrick's Day-themed project that serves as the artist's debut studio album and major-label debut. The album showcases a mix of energetic production, memorable hooks, and storytelling, reflecting both creative ambition and a commitment to developing a unique sound. With a growing catalog and a passion for creating music from the ground up, haxer23 continues to push forward as an artist who writes, produces, and performs with authenticity. Every release is another step in building a lasting presence in hip-hop while representing Yelm, Washington, on a larger stage.
